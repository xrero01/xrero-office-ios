2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Xrero Office is a free, open-source office suite (documents, spreadsheets, presentations) for iPhone, iPad and
Apple-silicon Mac. It is made for Arabic-speaking users, mainly in the United Arab Emirates and the Gulf, and for anyone
who needs to open, edit and save Microsoft Office files (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx) on the go. Many mobile office apps handle
right-to-left Arabic text poorly, require an account or a subscription, or upload documents to a server. Xrero Office
handles Arabic and right-to-left layout correctly, includes Arabic fonts, needs no account, works offline and keeps
every file on the device.

3. HOW TO USE (no login, no account, no in-app purchases)
- Launch the app: the document browser opens (Recents / Shared / Browse).
- Tap + (Create Document) and choose Document, Spreadsheet or Presentation; or open any .docx, .xlsx or .pptx file
  from Files.
- In the editor, type and format with the toolbar, then tap Save in the editor's top bar. The back arrow returns to
  the documents.
- The EN/AR switch in the editor header changes the interface language between English and Arabic.
- Sample files (English and Arabic document, spreadsheet and presentation):
  https://github.com/xrero01/xrero-office-ios/tree/main/store/samples

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app does not use any server, data provider, authentication service, payment processor, analytics,
advertising or AI service. All editing and file conversion run on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions; the interface is available in English and Arabic.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. The app is built on open-source software it is licensed to redistribute: the ONLYOFFICE

GNU GPL v2 with font exception, Bitstream Vera). All licences are shown in the app (iOS Settings > Xrero Office >
Licences), and the complete source code is published at https://github.com/xrero01/xrero-office-ios as the AGPL
